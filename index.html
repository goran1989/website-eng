How to Read

Select the colors of each band on your resistor from left to right. The first two bands are digits, third is the multiplier, and fourth shows tolerance.

🎯
Pro Tip

Hold the resistor with the tolerance band (usually gold or silver) on the right. Read from left to right for accurate color identification.
