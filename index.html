Platform Overview

The Noble Institute Job Mediation Platform is designed to bridge the gap between graduated students and potential employers. It provides a structured environment for career development, job searching, and professional networking.

Graduated Student

Alumni of Noble Institute who use the platform to find employment, manage their professional profile, and connect with employers.

Admin (Institute Staff)

Institute personnel who manage the platform, verify graduates, curate job postings, and facilitate connections.

Employer (External)

Organizations seeking talent, who post job opportunities and interact with graduated students through the platform.

Key Functionalities

The platform offers comprehensive features for each user type, including graduate verification, CV management, job search tools, application tracking, and networking opportunities.

Relationships & Dependencies

The use cases demonstrate include and extend relationships that show how functionalities depend on each other. For example, applying to a job requires managing CV versions, and tracking applications can trigger notifications.

Platform Features

Graduate Verification

Admin verification ensures only legitimate Noble Institute graduates can access the platform.

CV Management

Create, edit, and maintain multiple versions of your CV for different job applications.

Smart Job Matching

Advanced search and filtering tools to find the most relevant job opportunities.

Application Tracking

Monitor the status of all your job applications in one convenient dashboard.

Employer Feedback

Receive constructive feedback from employers to improve your future applications.

Networking Events

Participate in exclusive networking events connecting alumni with potential employers.
